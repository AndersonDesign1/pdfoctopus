Riverton Community Newsletter

Library hours

The library on Sample Road opens an hour earlier on weekdays from next month. Volunteers will run a reading club for children every Saturday morning, and the quiet room upstairs is now open until eight in the evening.

Books borrowed before the change can be returned at either desk. Late fees are waived for the first two weeks while the new system settles in.

Park clean-up

Thanks to everyone who came to the park clean-up last weekend. Forty people filled sixty bags of litter, planted twelve young trees along the river path and repainted the benches near the playground.

The next clean-up is planned for the spring. Gloves and tools are provided, and hot drinks will be served at the bandstand from ten o'clock.

Market news

The Saturday market moves to the old station yard while the square is being repaved. Stalls will open at eight, and parking is free in the north car park for the whole of the works.

New traders are welcome. Ask at the market office for a form; the first month's pitch fee is halved for anyone selling food grown or made within ten miles of the town.

Bus timetable

From the first of the month, the number seven bus runs every twenty minutes during the day and every hour after seven in the evening. The last bus from the station leaves at half past eleven.

Printed timetables are available at the library and the town hall.
